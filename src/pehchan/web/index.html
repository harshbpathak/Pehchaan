02 / How it works

Agents propose. The graph decides.

  1. 1

    Recall

    Has this wording been seen before? A FalkorDB vector index over past submissions answers instantly through the edges the agent wrote last time.

    FalkorDB vector index
  2. 2

    Scout

    Find recorded wordings by meaning, in many languages, then check both directions of entailment before calling anything a match.

    Multilingual embeddings · NLI
  3. 3

    Trace

    Follow claim → narrative frame → related claim → fact-check → publisher, plus native BFS and bounded paths.

    Cypher · algo.BFS
  4. 4

    Widen

    No direct match? Walk through the entities and frames the message shares with reviewed claims. Rare connections count more than hubs.

    Graph Detective
  5. 5

    Decide

    Known recycled claim, variant of a known narrative, or new. Draft a reply from graph records only, or queue it for a fact-checker.

    Evidence & Response
  6. 6

    Remember

    Write the message back into the graph, linked to what it matched, so the next forward is answered by following an edge.

    Submission write-back

04 / Proof

We measured it.

Held-out retrieval, 30 queries

24 paraphrases and 6 translations (Hindi, Spanish, German, French, Indonesian) that are not in the graph. A hit is a recorded wording of the same claim in the top five.

Methodhit@5Translations
Word overlap25/303/6
FalkorDB vector index30/306/6
Vector index + graph30/306/6

Embeddings already find every case here, so the graph adds no retrieval gain on this set. Its job is widening, recall and explanation.

What the graph does that search cannot

  • Links a vaccine-microchip claim to a climate claim through Bill Gates in four hops.
  • Answers a repeated forward through a MATCHES edge the agent wrote, without rerunning models.
  • Finds reviewed claims for a new wording through shared entities and frames, weighted by rarity.
  • Shows every edge's basis: source record, rule inference or model assessment.

05 / Connect your AI

Pehchan is an MCP server. The public endpoint is read-only: six tools for searching, tracing and assembling dossiers. Nothing is saved.
