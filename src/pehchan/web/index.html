00 / The rule

An agent that cites before it replies. Every claim it makes is a FalkorDB query: a matching record, the rating its publisher gave, and the path from that record to your message.

Fig 0.1

Match, or no claim

db.idx.vector.queryNodes finds recorded wordings by meaning, in any language. An NLI model must then agree in both directions before a record counts as the same claim. No match, no citation.

Fig 0.2

Context, not evidence

With no direct match the detective widens through shared entities and frames. Rarer nodes weigh more (1/degree); hits that share only a hub like 5G, or only a place, are dropped. Nothing widened is ever cited as evidence.

Fig 0.3

Remember on the graph

A saved case writes the message back as a Submission with MATCHES edges. A reworded repeat is answered through those edges in seconds, without rerunning the models, and says so.

03 / How it works

Agents propose. The graph decides.

Three specialists suggest; a read-only FalkorDB query decides what counts as a match, what is only context, and what gets remembered.

  1. 01

    Recall

    Has this wording been seen before? A FalkorDB vector index over past submissions answers instantly through the edges the agent wrote last time.

    FalkorDB vector index
  2. 02

    Scout

    Find recorded wordings by meaning, in many languages, then check both directions of entailment before calling anything a match.

    Multilingual embeddings · NLI
  3. 03

    Trace

    Follow claim → narrative frame → related claim → fact-check → publisher, plus native BFS and algo.SPpaths.

    Cypher · algo.BFS · algo.SPpaths
  4. 04

    Widen

    No direct match? Walk through the entities and frames the message shares with reviewed claims. Rare connections count more than hubs; places alone never count.

    Graph Detective
  5. 05

    Decide

    Known recycled claim, variant of a known narrative, or new. Draft a reply from graph records only, or queue it for a fact-checker.

    Evidence & Response
  6. 06

    Remember

    Write the message back into the graph, linked to what it matched, so the next forward is answered by following an edge.

    Submission write-back

Embeddings already find every case here, so the graph adds no retrieval gain on this set. Its job is widening, recall and explanation. Measured on the 248-variant graph; not rerun since the 2026 import.

What the graph does that search cannot

  • Links a vaccine-microchip claim to a climate claim through Bill Gates in four hops, in 0.05 s with algo.SPpaths.
  • Answers a repeated forward through a MATCHES edge the agent wrote, without rerunning models.
  • Finds reviewed claims for a new wording through shared entities and frames, weighted by rarity.
  • Shows every edge's basis: source record, rule inference or model assessment.
